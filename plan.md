# Fixive Website - Concept Preview & Waitlist Landing Page (Antigravity Plan)

> **Brand:** Fixive (Commercial Kitchen Preventive Maintenance & Repair Concept)  
> **Target Audience:** Restaurant Owners, Operational Managers, Executive Chefs, Dark Kitchen Fleets in KSA  
> **Current Status:** Pre-Launch / Concept Stage — Collecting Early Interest & Conducting Market Research  
> **Hosting Target:** GitHub Pages (Custom Domain via `CNAME`)

---

## 1. Landing Page Objective & Tone

To introduce **Fixive** as a proposed tech-driven solution for commercial kitchen maintenance in Saudi Arabia while remaining fully compliant during the pre-incorporation/pre-licensing phase.

**Compliance & Tone Guidelines:**
* **No Unlicensed Operations Claims:** Clearly state that Fixive is currently in development and not yet offering commercial services.
* **No Unverified Regulatory Claims:** Avoid claiming official authorization or endorsement from Balady, SFDA, or government entities. Frame these as platform design standards being built toward.
* **Focus on Interest & Research:** Frame interactions as market research, waitlist registration, and concept feedback calls rather than paid client onboarding or immediate dispatches.

## 2. Page Structure & Customer Journey

### Section 1: Top Navigation
* **Brand Logo:** `Fixive` (with modern mark)
* **Status Badge:** `⚪ Concept Phase • Waitlist Now Open`
* **Nav Links:** The Vision, How It Works, Waitlist Perks, Contact
* **CTA Button:** `Join Waitlist`

### Section 2: Hero Section (Concept & Vision)
* **Headline:** *"Rethinking Commercial Kitchen Maintenance in Saudi Arabia."*
* **Subheadline:** *"Say goodbye to unexpected combi oven failures and long technician delays. Fixive is developing a tech-enabled platform designed for scheduled preventive maintenance and streamlined repair management."*
* **Status Callout Banner:** *"ℹ️ Concept & Development Stage: Fixive is currently building its platform and inviting F&B operators to join our launch waitlist."*
* **CTAs:** `Join Launch Waitlist` | `Explore Platform Concept`

### Section 3: The Challenge & The Proposed Solution
* **Industry Challenges Today:**
  * Frequent, unexpected equipment downtime during peak hours.
  * Difficulty tracking repair histories across multiple kitchen units.
  * Lack of centralized maintenance visibility for multi-branch operators.
* **The Proposed Fixive Solution (In Development):**
  * **Scheduled Maintenance Frameworks:** Designed to help prevent emergency failures.
  * **Digital Equipment Logs:** QR-code-based asset history tracking.
  * **Streamlined Dispatch Workflows:** A tech-first approach to connecting kitchens with certified technicians upon commercial launch.

### Section 4: Interactive Concept Preview
* **Interactive Dashboard Preview:** A mock UI preview showcasing how the proposed platform will function:
  * **Tab 1: QR Asset Scanning** (Concept preview of scanning equipment tags to view history).
  * **Tab 2: Maintenance Request Flow** (Concept preview of logging an issue digitally).
  * **Tab 3: Preventive Schedule Tracker** (Concept preview of upcoming maintenance calendars).

### Section 5: Launch Waitlist Benefits
Transparently explain what early waitlist members receive prior to commercial launch:
* **Priority Access:** First in line for service availability upon official launch and licensing.
* **Free Initial Assessment:** Complimentary baseline kitchen health check once operations officially commence.
* **Product Input:** Opportunity to share operational feedback to help shape the software features.
